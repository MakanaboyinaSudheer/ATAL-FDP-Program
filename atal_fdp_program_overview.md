### 3. Program Objectives
1. **Explainable AI (XAI):** Develop expertise in AI and XAI to build transparent, trustworthy, and intelligent engineering solutions.
2. **Materials Informatics:** Introduce machine learning and Graph Neural Networks for materials analysis, property prediction, and accelerated materials discovery.
3. **Autonomous Drone Systems:** Build practical skills in AI-enabled UAVs, computer vision, navigation, obstacle detection, surveying, mapping, and multi-agent coordination.
