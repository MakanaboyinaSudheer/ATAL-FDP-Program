About the Programme

Where intelligent algorithms meet materials and machines

This AICTE ATAL Academy sponsored programme equips faculty, research scholars and industry professionals with the knowledge and practical skills to apply modern AI across two frontier domains: materials discovery and autonomous aerial systems. Explainable AI is the common thread, so the solutions you build stay transparent and trustworthy.

Lakireddy Bali Reddy College of Engineering

Mylavaram, Andhra Pradesh · Department of AI & Data Science

Autonomous NAAC 'A' Grade NBA Tier-1 Certified

Programme Objectives

  1. Explainable AI (XAI)

    Develop expertise in AI and XAI to build transparent, trustworthy and intelligent engineering solutions.

  2. Materials Informatics

    Machine learning and Graph Neural Networks for materials analysis, property prediction and accelerated materials discovery.

  3. Autonomous Drone Systems

    Practical skills in AI-enabled UAVs: computer vision, navigation, obstacle detection, surveying, mapping and multi-agent coordination.

How to Register

Secure your seat in four steps

Registration is handled only through the official AICTE ATAL Academy portal. It is completely free, with no charge for registration, course materials or certification.

Go to atalacademy.aicte-india.org

This page never collects payments. Beware of unofficial registration links.

  1. 1

    Create or sign in to your account

    Visit the AICTE ATAL Academy portal and log in, or sign up as a new participant.

  2. 2

    Find this FDP

    Search for "AI-Driven Materials Intelligence and Autonomous Systems" under the thrust area Advanced Computing.

  3. 3

    Apply for the programme

    Check that the host is LBRCE, Mylavaram and the dates are 14–19 Dec 2026, then submit your application.

  4. 4

    Save the dates

    Add the sessions to your calendar and watch for joining details from the organisers.
